# Target Companies Guide

Build a **10-15 company** list before you mass-apply. This list feeds warm intros, Job effort score, and your weekly apply plan. It is not a pile of Easy Apply clicks.

---

## Do this first - title and constraints

You need one primary title before the company list is useful.

## Do this

![Process: title, live posts, company reviews, tiers, write 10-15 names](assets/target-companies/process.png)

1. Confirm primary title + constraints (Job Target above)  
2. Open 2-3 live job posts for that title  
3. Split must-have vs nice-to-have duties  
4. Research interest on Glassdoor (or similar): Reviews + star breakdown  
5. Score companies in **Rank by what matters** (below). Tiers auto-fill Stretch / Stepping / Sandbox  
6. Override a tier if needed, then put every name in your tracker when Tracker Setup is done  

Time check: ~30 min for a top-of-mind list; ~1.5 hrs with light research. If it takes much longer, you are overthinking.

---

## Read live posts

Open **2-3** current postings for your title (employer careers page, Wellfound, LinkedIn, niche board).

| Keep as must-have | Treat as nice-to-have |
|-------------------|------------------------|
| Core duties you must already meet to be a real candidate | Tools, years, “nice if”, buzzwords |
| True deal-breakers (auth, location, clearance) | Wishlist bullets |

Copy 3-5 must-have duties into Brainstorm. You will reuse them in Job effort score and Compensation Planning later.

---

<h2 id="glassdoor-research-interest-check">Glassdoor research (interest check)</h2>

Glassdoor (or Levels.fyi / Blind / company careers pages) helps you answer: **Would I accept this company if they offered?** Not just “is the title right?”

1. Search the company name  
2. Open the **Reviews** tab  
3. Click into the **stars / ratings breakdown** (not only the overall number)  
4. Skim recent reviews for role families close to yours (data, eng, analytics)  

Write interest (high / medium / low), one green flag, and one red flag in the Rank spreadsheet below.

**Example (directional):** Google work/life balance near ~4.2 vs Tesla near ~2.9. Use **your** priorities: work/life balance, culture, career growth, pay, remote policy. Numbers move over time; re-check before you invest a full tailor.

![Glassdoor: Google ratings (Work/Life Balance about 4.2)](assets/target-companies/glassdoor-google-ratings.png)

![Glassdoor: Tesla ratings (Work/Life Balance about 2.9)](assets/target-companies/glassdoor-tesla-ratings.png)

If Glassdoor is behind a login wall, use the company careers page, LinkedIn employee posts, or a short warm ask: “What is it like on the data team?”

---

## Rank by what matters

Score each company on the traits that matter to **you** (work/life balance, culture, growth, pay, remote, or your own). Set weights 1-5. Fit scores recalculate and re-sort. Track interest, green/red flags, and URL in the same sheet.

Same spreadsheet on Progress: [Company Fit worksheet](../?ws=company-fit).

<div id="company-fit-writein" data-testid="guide-company-fit"></div>

Fit auto-ranks this sheet: top ~3 Stretch, middle Stepping, bottom ~3 Sandbox. Unscored names sit in Stepping. Override **Tier** on a row. Syncs to Module 4 Setup 1 (read-only company list) and Brainstorm display. When a real posting appears, score **Job effort** (Core / Proof / Access) on the same Company Fit row.
