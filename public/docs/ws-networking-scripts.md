**Networking Scripts**

**Fill this in the [Progress app](https://elliotastern.github.io/mentoring-progress/)** (autosaves). This page is the short copy bank only.

Guide: [Job Search Overview v8](overview-v8.md) · Chart A: connect **without** a note → message after accept · Follow-up apps **once** at **7-10 business days**.

## Default rules (Overview v8)

1. **LinkedIn connection requests: no note.** Connect first → message second (~10% reply after accept vs ~3% with a note on the request).
2. **Application follow-up:** one short note at **7-10 business days**, then move on (two-week silence is often normal).
3. **Cold email:** secondary only - cap **5-10/week**; never your primary channel.
4. **Spend hours on Chart A ranks 1-4** (inbound LinkedIn, warm/referral, after-connect message, careers page). Easy Apply ≈ 0 hours.

Paste and tweak the Overview templates below. Do **not** put a pitch on the connection request.
