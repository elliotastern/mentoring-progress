# 4 Most Common Interview Questions

## “Tell me about yourself?” (Or some variation)

### Rough Template:

### Who you are

In one sentence introduce yourself and your current role.
E.g.* “I am a Data Scientist with a background in Software Engineering and Statistics and am currently the data science manager at ABC company.”*
### Your Highlight reel

Use the next 2-3 sentences to make yourself stand out. Talk about your accomplishments/strengths and talk of how they relate with the role. Remember to quantify your achievements to add credibility.

E.g*. “In my previous position, I was promoted 3 times within 3 months each.  I developed a machine learning solution that saved the company over $200k in a month. I Managed benchmarks, metrics and improvement of end-to-end ML pipelines to predict order completion for customer churn responsible for $2M+ in revenue per year. Spearheaded Python, Git, SDLC standards and data reproducibility best practices to reduce time to production by 50%.*

### Why you’re here

Use the next 1-3 sentences to show how the role and the company are great opportunities.

For example, “*This opportunity caught my attention because what you’re building in the data science space is exactly in line with my passion and expertise. With my range of experience, I see this as a chance for me to make an impact quickly at the company, I also see this as a major opportunity for my growth as the team scales*
