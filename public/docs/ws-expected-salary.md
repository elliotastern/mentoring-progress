## Ladder (edit to your numbers)

1. **Deflect early:** ask for their range; learn the role and total package first.  
2. **Market range:** "For [title] in [city], I'm seeing about [low]-[high]."  
3. **Floor-anchored ask:** "My floor is about $[minimum]. Happy to talk total package once we've confirmed fit."

## Rules

- Anchored to research + walk-away line you already wrote  
- Do not invent a lowball on the first ask  
- Practice out loud once before the next recruiter screen
