# What Are Bumps

**Bumps** are the fears, challenges, and failures one is likely to face in life
# Importance Of Bumps

- There is a meaning to every failure in life.**

Failures are delays, not defeats. After all, to train machine learning models, we need examples of failure. If all training data is successful, the model would be unable to learn. Similarly, in life, failures and challenges are loaded with essential lessons that if well used, can stir up our growth and allow us to reach our goals

- Failure and success are not opposites**
Every success and failure you encounter in life, is guiding you in one direction. That is your goal. Failures and successes both allow you to achieve your goals and vision. Many of the world’s biggest successes come with large amounts of failure or learning. The KFC recipe was rejected over 1,000 times before it was accepted. These failures were just along the path to success.

- Bumps help us to achieve our goals**
Bumps are just setbacks and carry variable lessons that help us achieve our goals. While planning your goals, also plan for bumps.

- Failure enhances creativity**
When we encounter challenges or failures, we start looking for solutions to help us overcome them. This process involves trying out new things and thinking out of the box which triggers creativity and innovation

- Bumps help you to learn new ways of coping with emotions**
Fears, challenges, and failures can cause you pain. This pain teaches you to focus on the positive things instead of the negative things.

- Bumps help to redefine your priorities**
Bumps remind us of the things we should be focusing on. This prevents diversion and allows us to stick to our priorities.

# Bumps Are Inevitable

Although we don’t plan nor wish for fears, problems, or failures they will always be a part of every journey.

If we know that there will be these fears, problems, and failures along the process, then why do we feel discouraged when we hit them?

Maybe because school teaches us that we should be punished when we fail, but that’s not how life teaches us.

Maybe because society teaches us to go to school, find a safe secure job and not take risks.

The truth is, we are **guaranteed** to face hard times in life and on every journey.

Embrace hard times because they help you learn, grow and improve.

Failures, fears, problems, and difficult times never have to be negative
You can’t let previous negative experiences hold you back from reaching your goals and dreams.

There is always a positive takeaway from each negative experience.

Challenges don’t determine our direction in life; they, they reveal our character. They show us how to respond.

“Character can not be developed with ease and quiet. Only through experience of trial and suffering can the soul be strengthened, vision cleared, ambition inspired, and success achieved,” Helen Keller.

There is a famous tradition in Japan for art called the **“art of kintsugi”**. It is where broken pottery is mended with gold so the cracks are illuminated rather than concealed.

This activity is done with a sense of duty and love to remember the failures and scars of life.

Putting the pieces together means you can always put your life back together after a failure. This can be done with patience, dedication, and persistence

Where the gold illumination represents the failures and scars of life that are beautiful and meaningful to grow.
A famous quote I try to live by every day is “Fail fast, fail often” by Elon Musk. This reminds me it is important to fail in all aspects of life!
The trick is the mindset. If you change your mindset, it no longer becomes a failure but rather a vital step in the process of achieving the goal.
# How To Overcome Bumps

- Expect bumps**
Life is not a straight line and no matter how well we plan, fears, challenges, and failures will always be there. There is, therefore, a need to expect and plan for bumps

- Acknowledge bumps when they occur**
Acknowledge and accept bumps when they occur and how they make you feel. This is an important step in the process of overcoming bumps.

- Be a researcher**
When you encounter bumps in life, you ought to do your research. Ask yourself what worked, what didn’t, and what resulted in the bump. Finding the root cause will help you to quickly overcome your bump

- Write down the lessons bumps teach you**
Every failure is an opportunity to learn. If there is something you want to carry from every failure, is the lessons. These lessons allow you to move on with life. They help you know what to avoid, what measures to put in place, and how to prepare for future circumstances.

- Find a path to progress**
You need to find a solution to every challenge and failure you encounter else it’s useless. I mean, what good will it do if bumps happen and you allow yourself to remain stuck?

You need to be practical. For example, if you failed to get a job because you failed the technical part of the interview which was about cracking a code, you need to start practicing coding daily and solving as many questions as possible before your next interview.

You do not want to repeat the same mistake twice.

- Manage your self-talk**
Do not sell doubt to your mind when you encounter a challenge. It can be the easiest thing to do but remember that negative thoughts can easily overpower your positive thoughts.

Experts say that the ratio of positive thoughts to negative thoughts should be 3:1. This means that one negative thought can be overcome by at least three positive thoughts.

If you want to easily overcome bumps, feed your mind with positive thoughts. See the positive in every negative experience

- Take a break if need be**
Challenges and failures can drain us so badly and unmotivate us. Imagine working so hard for it to be futile. It’s overwhelming, right?

I encourage people to take breaks when necessary. Your body is like a car. If you don’t service your car, it will not work as expected. If you don’t take time to reflect, relax, and recharge, bumps will overcome you and you will not deliver on the end results i.e. achieve your goals

- Gratitude**
Practicing gratitude helps us more easily overcome adversity such as failures. Gratitude, focusing on the positives in our lives, is a great balancer to the failures we will encounter when reaching for a difficult goal. As our minds feel negative events are about 3x as powerful as positive events, we greatly overrate the amount of negativity in our lives in what psychologists call a negativity bias. The negativity bias is quite intelligent because by focusing on our weaknesses, we are more likely to solve them. We can see this from an algorithm perspective with self-driving as Tesla finds and corrects the point where their cars make a mistake to improve their performance. While this is great for learning, this can take a toll psychologically. Practicing gratitude is a great way to balance out our focus on failure by adding our focus on the positives.

Here are a few ways to practice gratitude:

*Thank someone mentally. *Thank somebody mentally is an easy, fast way to add some gratitude to your life.
*Keep a gratitude journal. *Writing down a few specific things you are thankful for daily is a great way to boost gratitude.
*Practice sharing gratitude with others.* Practicing sharing a few specific things you are thankful for each day will both increase gratitude and build your relationship with the person you share your gratitude with.
*Writing thank-you letters. *Writing an email or letter is a fantastic chance to increase gratitude and add to your relationships.

# How To Use Failure As Opportunities

“Failure is an opportunity to begin again, this time more intelligently,” Henry Ford.

Do not sweep away failure. Use it to re-evaluate your strategy. Find out what went wrong and what you can do to make it right.

Look at people like Jeff Bezos. How many years did it take for Amazon to be profitable? Did he have the option to give up? But he didn’t right? If he gave up, would we be using Amazon today?

Use the stones challenges and failures throw at you to build strong foundations that will lead you to not only achieve your goals but also your vision.

# Bonus: Planning For Failures - Activity

We are interested in all of your goals but you are not forced to share anything that you are not comfortable with. It is encouraged for the mentee to show your mentor this activity for the main goal of this mentorship. You can try it out [here](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1cY_oBLAE6mW-TamYaC4SnpwDWbxlsoVp4PkGhGkJQbE/edit). - Write down your biggest fears that will arise on this journey to our goal.
- For each fear, reflect, think and write down why these are fears for you.
Scientifically there are only two fears that humans are born with, fear of falling and fear of loud noises. Thus, every other fear comes from a person, situation, or experience in your life. Remember this when you write your reasons why.
- Choose one goal (preferably the main goal of this mentorship). Then write what failures you think will happen on this journey. Try to focus on the inevitable, not what could possibly happen. If you would like to do it for all of your goals in life, that’s great!
- Write down all the previous negative experiences that you can remember in your life or previous negative experiences related to the main goal of this mentorship.
- For each negative experience, write down something that you can take away from it as a positive outcome.
- Now for each related negative experience write how you will feel and react if or when they occur in this journey.
- BONUS: List the 5 biggest events in your life that had the greatest impact on you (positively or negatively)
- Lastly, for each fear, failure, or negative experience, write how it will help you on your journey to your goal and vision.
