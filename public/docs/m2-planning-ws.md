## Is the problem descriptive, diagnostic, predictive or prescriptive analytics? These are steps thus, some are a subset of the next steps. As they can be pretty big for each. (Definitions below) Then explain in detail.

- Descriptive **- Describes what happened in the past
- Diagnostic **- Describes why something happened in the past
- Predictive** - Predicts what is most likely to happen in the future
- Prescriptive **- Suggests actions you can take to affect the predicted outcomes
