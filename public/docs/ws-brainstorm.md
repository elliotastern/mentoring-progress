# Goal:

Ideally, we want **10-15 companies** you are interested in (3 - stretch, 4-9 - Stepping stones, 3 - Sandbox).

- Stretch **- Companies you’d love to work for (a.k.a your dreams)
- Stepping Stone** - Companies you’d be excited about that offer more experience
- Sandbox**
  - Companies that you qualify for and can practice (networking and in the job) in a low-stake environment
  - Companies you are going to experiment with (the mindset is you don’t care about getting rejected from this company)
  - So you can test the best methods and find out what works for you
  - We are going to start off with these companies

Additionally, research (Google or LinkedIn are good places to start) will prepare you for when we want to start doing cold messages to employers.

# Definitions:

Before we start, let’s define some key terms

**Sector** - Often general terms of recognized groups, holds several industries and markets. Each has unique characteristics

E.g. Financial, Public Sector, services, Healthcare, Energy, Communications, Technology, Agriculture, etc

**Industry** - Can be defined by any general business activity or commercial enterprise, frequently named after its primary product

E.g. Banking, Insurance, Renewables, Manufacturing, Aerospace, Clothing, Cosmetics, Electronics, Creative, etc

**Market (Niche) -** a group of customers who require the products and services provided by an industry

E.g For banks: potential customers i.e first-time buyers
