# Pre-interview Checklist

(I only spend ~5-10 mins):

- Add To Tracker - **Sheet 5 in your [Job Tracker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/d/1V0nwQSdzex4NIz1En9hU1UnXG2E_a7s2VRNmHKDgHDE/edit)
- Job Post Review** - Review the job posting and study it as a cheat sheet

  - Get Job Description (JD)
  - Review JD - extract common terms and phrases (use it as a cheat sheet)
  - List at least 10 reasons why you are a great ______ (Job title)
  - Use ChatGPT (Input: JD + resume) - to make questions and remind me why my resume is a good fit for the role
  - Make bullets and scripts
  - Practice those questions
  - Tweak & revise (Review Module 5 for extra tips)
  - Review questions from previous interviews (Double down on ones that went well and improve on the ones that went poorly - hopefully you added them to your job tracker so you can reflect on them)
  - Then take bullets and script and make a good story
  - BONUS TIP**: The best interviews use the same story for multiple questions. Why? It saves you time in explaining and you can show more impact and value to the interviewer

- Conversation Review** - Review the conversations you had with the hiring manager/HR/Recruiter to recall any additional information that was given
- Research interviewers **- understand who is interviewing you
  - The easiest way is to use LinkedIn by searching the company and then finding people who work there and match with an email list or calendar invite names
  - Understand their background (technical vs non-technical)
  - Their history with the company (short vs long-time employee)
- Prepare your questions **- 3-5 good questions ([examples](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-g1neq8G9bXSL3qh_U5eG12ym328IyTcwZ62xxw9uwU/edit?usp=share_link))
- “Look good, feel good”** - Dress professionally even if a virtual meeting
- Show up on time - **No need to be super early and there are actually some reasons why not to show up too early but with virtual interviews, those reasons are minimized. 5 - 10 minutes prior is good enough
- (Virtually) Check Connection **- Open a test meeting with the software used for the interview. (You might need to download ie MS Teams)
- (Virtually) Test Mic, video** - Configure or connect the correct mic.
- Note-Taking Ready** - Unless you have an amazing memory I suggest taking notes (mainly on things they tell you not what they ask you)
- Remember it is just a casual conversation**
- Remember to have fun**
- Remind yourself why you are a great fit for the role **(Give at least 3 reasons)
