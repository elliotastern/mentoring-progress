## What Does This Mentorship Provide:

- Mindset, Perspective or Emotional Shift **- The mentor will aid the mentee in changing how the mentee views a particular situation. This is very vague and I realize this but you can consider it as support or talking/thinking out problems. I am very talented at this, it is my biggest asset.

- Accountability **- The mentor and mentee will agree on deadlines for all following meetings. The mentor will check in, motivate and encourage the mentee whenever deemed fit. With that said mentees should **not **wait for the following meeting or check in if they are stuck, open communication is preferred.

- Support **- Continuous communication, via direct messaging. With that said we all have busy lives and I am pretty good at answering messages but let’s agree on a 48-hour response before either party sends a reminder.

- Community** - If there are any other mentees in the program at the time they are welcome and advised to group up for projects, practice interviews and for additional network support.

- Time and Energy Management **- The mentor will provide tips and strategies to assess the mentee's time and energy management if the mentee requires it.

- Roadmap planning **- The mentor and mentee will agree on the planning for X amount of weeks during the mentorship program.

- Goal Planning Guidance **- The mentor will guide the mentee's path post-mentorship program to any goal(s) they wish.

- Resume Review **- The Mentor will provide resources and support for resumes and will guarantee **2 **resume reviews with in-depth comments. Afterwards, the responsibility of continuously tweaking/improving the resume is up to the mentee.

- Cover Letter Review **- The Mentor will provide resources and support for resumes and will guarantee **2 **cover letter reviews with in-depth comments. Afterwards, the responsibility of continuously tweaking/improving the cover letter is up to the mentee.

- LinkedIn Review **(or other social media if preferred) - The mentor will provide resources and support to optimize social media accounts to get more traffic and keep leads interested.

- GitHub Review **(and/or personal website) - The mentor will provide resources and support to optimize code, inference and projects. The mentor will review **pull requests **a guaranteed **once **per week with a **48-hour response rate**. **NOTE: **the weekend will be the best time to review code, thus having the pull request by 4 pm EDT on Friday, is highly suggested.

- Job Application Optimization **- The Mentor will provide resources and support on how to optimize the mentee’s application process to be the most effective and efficient with their time and energy.

- Project Planning **(If applicable) - Where the mentor aids and guides a project applicable and specific to the mentee’s industry. The mentee is responsible for the project work.

- Technical/Topic/Concepts Review **- Read this [link](https://mentorcruise.com/blog/whats-difference-between-teacher-vs-mentor/)for the difference between teacher and mentor

- Code review **(If applicable) - The mentor will provide code review in languages that they are familiar with (Preferably: Python. R and SQL are included as well) if the mentee requires it. This will be ideally done via pull/merge requests. This does **not **guarantee the mentor will provide debugging support.

- Presentation and Storytelling of Data **- This will include tips and strategies for presentations as well as how to display interpretations and inferences from experiments.

- Interview Preparation **- The mentor will provide tips on how to prepare for interviews, including questions, what to refresh and how to sell yourself. Mentor will provide general information and will guarantee a **few **example walk-throughs if the mentee requires it. **Note: There will be a small to-do list to complete before your first interview prep call, and this is so we can maximize the calls by improving some skills before the call. **

- Mock Interviews** - Where the mentor replicates an interview from said company. **Note: There will be a small to-do list to complete before your first interview mock call, and this is so we can maximize the calls by improving some skills before the call. **

- Networking **- Introduction to any connection in my network plus additional inquiries if reasonable.

- Potential Company Referrals **- The mentor will potentially provide a referral to their own company if the mentor deems them a good fit.

- Contract Negotiations **- The mentor will provide general tips and strategies and aid the mentee in offering discussions **unless** the mentor has a *conflict of interest *(ie the mentor is interviewing the mentee for their current company).

If a topic is not on the list just ask, I might be able to help, use my network or gather some resources.

I personally want to help everyone and I am confident that I can help everyone in some way but it starts with you. [Do you want to change?](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1ILtiF78bDJ33pq-_fbriTyX_cAiJcRXZrY-zdgk02kM/edit) You control your own life, take the leap and strive for your goals.

There is no luck in success! The “luck” that people often say is when **opportunity **meets **preparation**!

I live by the phrase  “**Extreme Ownership**” (If you are not familiar with the book *Extreme Ownership: How U.S. Navy Seals Lead and Win**** ***by Jocko Willink, I highly suggest you check it out)

This lifestyle is saying that you are in control of everything in your life!

Not everything is your fault in life but it is your responsibility to how you **feel **about a situation, what you **do **in a situation, and the **choices **you make in a situation and it is your **responsibility **to fix or improve the situation.

- Lost your job => not your fault but it is up to you to find another source of income.

- Rejected for the 50th time => You control how you feel about those rejections and it is your responsibility to improve and reduce the chance of getting rejected again.

- “They tell me I need more experience” => Take action in showcasing your experience better

I can empathize with you in some situations but I don’t do well with excuses in blaming external issues “The economy is bad”, “the job market is bad” and “The pandemic has ruined the job market”...

Life happens, we adjust our path and keep pushing toward our goals. Once you take extreme ownership of every negative thing in your life, you will only focus on improving yourself to better prepare yourself for the next opportunity.
