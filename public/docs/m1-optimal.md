**Recall: **Reverse Engineered job search

- Accept an offer ( ~ 3 days)
- Contract(s) Negotiation ( ~ 1 week)
- Receive multiple offers around the same time (**GOAL:** 4 offers simultaneously )
- Final interviews ( ~ 2 weeks)
- Intermediate Application stages ( ~ 1 week)
- Screening/Behavioural Interviews ( ~ 1 week)
- Applying or Networking (Applying: ~ 1-4 weeks)
- Optimizing Portfolio (Depends on the individual)
Thus, the goal (in the average case) is to start applying in week 5 or 6, if not already.

If your goal is “now” for when you want to start your next position I suggest, if you haven’t already started to apply and gather data on yourself then I would start doing this, that way we can see where we need to improve as a candidate.

**The job search is just an experiment, here's why:**

- Recruiters/Employers aren't coming to you? Tweak your LinkedIn.
- People aren't replying? Tweak your outreach message.
- Employers are instantly rejecting you? Tweak your resume.
- Employers are saying "not enough experience"? Tweak your GitHub.
- Employers don't see your non-tech skills? Tweak your cover letter.
- Not seeing results in your job search? Tweak your method.

You're a Data Scientist now, we don't do experiments based on emotions, only on the data.
