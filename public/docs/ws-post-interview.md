## Reflection on Self

After every interview, it is important to reflect. Take a few breaths, go for a walk or walk around your house and ask yourself.

- What did I do well?
- Think 5 minimum - but remember the ratio of positive to negative thoughts to make a difference in your overall well-being is 3. So make sure the positive thoughts are 3 times the negative!
- It doesn’t even have to be what you said. Did you look professional? Did you show up on time? Did you review the job posting prior to the interview?
- What did you not do so well?
- What could you improve next time?
- Each Interview question - Save the question (ie [Job Tracker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/d/1V0nwQSdzex4NIz1En9hU1UnXG2E_a7s2VRNmHKDgHDE/edit) in  Sheet 5) that they asked you and then do each of the above 3 questions and make notes and comments.
  - If you didn’t answer the question then prepare or find out the correct question.
  - If you think you can improve it then, tweak your answer
  - If you think you got it correct, confirm it online or with someone

**NOTE**: This helps you remember what to improve for next time. My suggestion would be to keep a log of all your interview experience (E.g. [Job Tracker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/d/1V0nwQSdzex4NIz1En9hU1UnXG2E_a7s2VRNmHKDgHDE/edit) in  Sheet 5 in the notes column, Google Docs, Google Sheets, in-person notebook, etc)

## Reflection on company/team/compensations

- Questions to ask yourself?
  - Were there any red flags
  - Am I interested in the work?
  - Am I interested in the team?
  - Am I interested in the environment/culture?
  - Do the job tasks align with my wants?
  - Does the salary align with my expected salary?
  - Do the benefits align with most of my complete compensation package wishes?
  - Is there growth in the team or company?
  - Do I like how the feedback system works?
  - Are they invested in my career growth or am I just a number to them?

## Commit

## **The Follow-up**

Follow up. Once an interview is over, send a message to the recruiter and, if possible, the interviewers. Thank them for their time and for anything you learned during the interview. If this particular job is at the top of your list, let them know. If you’ve already made that clear, say it again.

If you get the job, congratulations! If you don’t, it may not be over yet. Tell the recruiter and anyone you met during the interview that you’re still interested. If you made a good impression, they may keep you in mind when other positions open up. I’ve been rejected only to get an offer a few months later. So stay in touch with everyone. Your effort may pay off down the line.

### Template:

Hey $NAME,

Thank you again for your time and for considering me for roles at [Company Name].

I left my interview with not only a positive experience but learned more about [what you learned about], which I [action you will do or excitement you have with proof why it matches your skills].

Looking forward to learning more from your expertise.

Thanks, Your Name
