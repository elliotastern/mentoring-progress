# What Is Measuring Impact?

People who get hired at top companies understand how to measure their impact. Hiring managers need to know the value you added to your previous organizations and what you will bring to the table when they hire you.

If they don’t know what you did and you don’t tell them, they won’t know.

Measuring impact is an effective way to demonstrate value and highlight achievements in prior job positions
The mantra for measuring impact is to “deliver more value than alternative options.” alternative options are not only other applicants but any other options available to the employer
These achievements and values leave a positive impression of you on the hiring manager. They help you stand out from the competition, therefore, increasing your chances of getting the job.

It’s all about showing the employer that you understand their needs best and you can solve them.
The employer oftentimes doesn’t care how you will get the job done, they care about the value you will add
For example,
**What not to say to hirers**: I will build this in C++.
This only focuses on the product. Use the feature to explain why it helps solve their problem.
**What to say to hirers**: I will build this in C++ because it will give you a faster application than competing technology.
By saying the why, we show the value that we can give, and the C++ part gives your argument credibility as it sounds advanced to nontechnical customers

## Gather Your Metrics

It is very important to keep impactful data of any organization you work for. The data will help you update your portfolio without much struggle.

If you find yourself in a situation where you lack impactful data, you need to shift your focus to gathering impactful metrics that relate to the position you are applying to.

Gather data on the areas you performed best that relate to the position you are applying for, then proceed to other areas as time allows.

## List Measurable Achievements

It is essential to quantify your achievements in precise figures.

For example, instead of writing that you “Automated a program that helped saved the organization time,” You can write, “I used Zapier(tool) to automate Trello and Slack (communication channels), which helped save the organization 3 hours daily. The saved hours were directed to performing other tasks, and this increased performance”

You see, now the job recruiter will have it clear that you saved your organization 3 hours daily. “3” is very particular.

If you don’t have precise numbers, then you are allowed to **make use of ranges** and **percentages**. Lack of specific data could be a result of you don’t have the data you need or there was a variance in underlying data

Using the example above, if you don’t know the accurate number of hours you can either write it as

“I used Zapier(tool) to automate Trello and Slack (communication channels) which helped save the organization 2-3 hours daily. The saved hours were directed to performing other tasks and this increased performance”

Or

“I used Zapier(tool) to automate Trello and Slack (communication channels) which helped save the organization 12% of office time daily. The saved hours were directed to performing other tasks and this increased performance”

Although the recruiter would have appreciated the specifics, they will have a clue of the amount of time you helped save.

# Resume Formula:

Rules
- Be specific
- Give context
- Focus on impact
Add statements that show the impact of your work. Instead of saying, “I did (X), say I did (X) thereby achieving ABC impact.”

- It's okay to solve a problem
- Don't downplay your work
- Add descriptive adjectives.
Every noun on your resume should be accompanied by a descriptive adjective that answers, “What Kind?” For example, “An output?” “What kind of output?” The words you will use to describe “The output” are what will make your results more credible and your resume impressive.

(1) (2)  (3)

“Led (1) the production release of a new, $2.8M software package that generated (2) $1.4M in revenue, and 250K new users within the first 6 months of release.  All 14 team members were awarded stock units in recognition of their performance (3)”

**TIP:** You can also use Google’s Resume bullet Format– "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z].” (Read here if you want more info on [Google’s X by Y by Z formula](https://www.inc.com/bill-murphy-jr/google-recruiters-say-these-5-resume-tips-including-x-y-z-formula-will-improve-your-odds-of-getting-hired-at-google.html))

# Good Questions To Help You Measure Impact

- What was there before you started?
  - Before you join the team
  - What code was there?
  - What was the team's efficiency?
  - What was the team’s collaboration?
  - What skills did teammates have?
- How did you go from where it was to where you left it (or is now)?
  - (If research) what was given by proof?
  - What was given by your boss?
- What ideas did you provide that you did not necessarily work on?
- Did you help teammates complete their tasks?
- What feedback have you gotten from your team or boss?
  - Think of every “thank you” you received
I highly suggest you have to create notes and write every time you have received feedback to track
  - This will help you write your portfolio in the future
  - Reflect on
  - Remind you why you are great
  - Measure your growth from the negative feedback
- What was the goal/scope of the task/project and what did you do extra?
- What best practices did you apply?
- What documentation did you add? Could all skill levels understand and complete this task?
- How do you run the task/project? (compare before to current)
- What makes the task/project unique?
- What can be done next? (thanks for your excellent work)
- How to measure the impact from A to B

When people work on a research project they often think, that if it wasn’t published then it doesn’t count as an impact

But if you relate this to the field when you finish a task/project or find a solution, it’s not industry-changing, it’s to solve a problem for the particular company. Thus, you made them go one step closer from A to B
