## Effort rule

| Total | Action |
|------:|--------|
| 8-10 | Full effort: tailor, referral, HM/recruiter note, attach proof |
| 6-7 | Targeted apply + one concise outreach |
| 0-5 | Usually skip (unless intentional practice) |
