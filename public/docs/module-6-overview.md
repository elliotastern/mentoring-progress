**What will be covered in this module:**
- Negotiations in general
  - What and how to negotiate in general
  - Common Mistakes
  - Do advantages affect future engagement
  - Preparing for the ask

- Negotiating The Complete Compensation Package
  - 3 most important concepts for negotiations
  - What is a compensation package
  - When do negotiations start
  - How to answer “What is your expected/desired salary”
  - How to value yourself in the market
  - Red flags
  - How to gain leverage
  - Mentor Advice
- Post Job Offer Acceptance
  - Tips for asking for a raise
  - Top three ways to get promoted
- Learning from Mistakes

In this module, there will be a [worksheet](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1BFxlL3UzwgHMyiwDAZshNS5Zn3Ab5b9GaaYcqL4nz-w/edit?usp=sharing) to interactively follow along.

Some of this module will be related to negotiations in general but if they are just try to think of it as your current negotiation situation.

A [bonus](https://drive.google.com/file/d/16oAgtOsgU01taxvLU2aw2RBhfB2IYtoq/view?usp=sharing)in this module is a PDF of how to respond to each type of offer via email, but they can be used to plan or practice your discussion/ask, it is very valuable.

The goal of this module is for you to find your value/worth and never settle for less and how to navigate the path.

Negotiations will not stop once your get this job but will continue throughout your career and life.
For this module, the slack channel to ask your questions is **#6-contract-negotiations**
