## Live Coding Interview

- Understand the [UMPIRE approach](https://guides.codepath.com/compsci/UMPIRE-Interview-Strategy) (10 mins) for coding assessments, coding or tech interviews
- Understand** what the interviewer is asking for by using test cases and questions about the problem.
- Match** what this problem looks like to known categories of problems, e.g. Linked List or Dynamic Programming and strategies or patterns in those categories.
- Plan** the solution with appropriate visualizations and pseudocode.
- Implement** the code to solve the algorithm.
- Review** the code by running specific example(s) and recording values (watchlist) of your code's variables along the way.
- Evaluate** the performance of your algorithm and state any strong/weak or future potential work.

- Complete [1 Week  Interview Prep Questions | HackerRank](https://www.hackerrank.com/interview/preparation-kits/one-week-preparation-kit/one-week-day-one/challenges)
- Watch [Software Engineering Job Interview – Full Mock Interview](https://youtu.be/1qw5ITr3k9E?si=_mvQLr_r5_3DJR7J) (75 mins)
- Complete (at least 10 questions - 8 easy and 2 medium) [Top Interview 150 - Study Plan - LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/studyplan/top-interview-150/) (Talk aloud, explaining your thought process)
- Record yourself completing a problem (Talk aloud, explaining your thought process)
- Watch the recording and take notes of
  - (1) what you did well
  - (2) What you didn’t do well

## Case Study

These are typically broad and company-specific, where you talk aloud breaking down a problem, and skill level for designing a problem.

They are also really good practice to solve any problem in life or your career.

- Understand at a high level how they work
  - Data Scientist - [Crack the Data Scientist Case Interview by an Ex-Google Data Scientist](https://youtu.be/DqgiUo44X-k?si=vOUIgKQJXKwbb7QH) (30 mins)
  - Data Analyst
  - Machine Learning Engineer
  - Data Engineer
  - Watch: [Top 10+ Data Engineer Interview Questions and Answers](https://youtu.be/svBO4nUDQDs?si=uYALYMmKxNzGcsSS&t=634) (10:34 to end) (4 mins)
  - Read: [Data Engineer Case Study Interview Guide](https://www.interviewquery.com/p/data-engineer-case-study) (10 mins)
- Use this framework:
  - Step 1: Clarify**
  - Ask clarifying questions to define the scope of the problem
  - Step 2: Constrain (based on reply from step 1)**
  - Constrain the open-ended problem into a thesis or key objective
  - Example
  - Based on your clarification, I will focus on… The purpose is to
  - I can tell $company cares about… then this affects the $company’s mission/goal/business model etc
  - Step 3: Plan **
  - Take a minute or two to frame your response (Say “I’d like to take a minute to gather my thoughts on the solution if that’s ok with you
  - Address your plan
  - “To address this problem, I would like to discuss…(mention set of things you would discuss) is that ok with you?
  - “To address this problem I would first discuss…. Then… finally I would like to conclude with the business recommendation or how this could be useful for the $company
  - Step 4: Method**
  - How would you solve the problem?
  - Define or provide a supportive answer to the set of things you mentioned in step 3 to discuss
  - Step 5: Conclude**
  - Provide solutions/recommendations and mention how they impact the business outcome
