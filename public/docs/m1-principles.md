# Importance Of Principles And Values

- They guide decision-making**
They help us make wise choices and stand for what is right.

- They help us relate with teams**
Good values and principles help us to treat other people better and even put us in a position to help them

- They guide our direction in life**
We know the right path to follow when we have defined principles and values. They also outline the consequences of following the wrong paths

- They build character**
Values like kindness and humility help to strengthen your character. They become the foundation of your moral convictions.

- Helps to boost confidence**
When you adopt quality behaviour and morals, you face people and circumstances more confidently and responsibly.

- They help to build mental strength to cope with stressful situations.**
Everyone is guaranteed to be faced with challenges in life. Principles and values help you discern how to handle challenges and give you the courage to do them.

# What Are Principles And Values

**Personal Values **- General terms that motivate people’s actions and serve as a guide to their lives. They are the character traits we develop and decide to live by to direct our daily behaviour and attitude
You use values to set your foundational principles.

Examples of Values
- Humility
- Loyalty
- Courage
- Fairness
- Honesty
- Patience
- Gratitude

**Principle **- This is a statement that you choose to live by that relates to your values. A principle is a personal rule that you decide to follow. Can also be defined as morals governing behaviour.

For example, if your value is** honesty**, your principle can be something like, **I will always be transparent **

# How To Set Principles And Values

- Self-reflect on your daily life**
- What guides you?
- How do you relate with people?
- What things despise you?
- How do you want to live moving forward?

- List down your values and principles**
- TIP: Google “list of values” and read them off to see if they sound like you.

- Reduce your list to 5 values that you feel represent you best**
- Write down what each of these values means to you and why they represent you
- Rank the 5 values in order of priority**
- 1 being the most prioritized and 5 being the least prioritized
- Define your principle**
- Write a statement that sums up your values and that you want to live by
# When To Use Your Values And Principles

The values and principles are great to reflect on when you are conflicted between what you should do or what you want to do.

Another use is to help you determine if a decision is morally/ethically right and if it aligns with your core values.

With this list, it’s easy to say “no” to tasks that don’t represent your core values.

# What If There Is A Conflict Between Two Values?

This is why we ranked them from 1 to 5, meaning the higher rank takes priority.

# What Do You Do With Your Principles And Values?

If you don’t want to do anything with it, that’s fine but still, be proud of yourself for taking the time to self-reflect and improve your self-awareness.

It is suggested to place the list of 5 values and principles somewhere you see it every day, such as on your wall in your room, on your phone lock screen, or on your daily to-do list. This will remind you of the values that you live by.

# What Happens When You Apply Your Principles And Values

- You will relate well with other people
- You will build your character
- You will be more confident
